Power Card · Vote or Fade Away

Start Here

Two minutes of setup, and the game lives on your home screen like an app — no app store, no account, nothing to buy. Find your phone below and follow the steps in order.

▶ Open Power Card

That button is the game. The steps below show how to keep it on your home screen.

iPhone From the link

Do this in Safari — the blue compass app that came with your phone.

  1. Tap Open Power Card above. The game opens in Safari.
    If it opened inside some other app instead, copy the game’s address — vote-or-fade-away.vercel.app — and paste it into Safari’s address bar at the top, then tap Go.
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top corner. Done!

Power Card now sits on your home screen next to your other apps. Open it from there from now on. Bonus: once it has opened online one time, it even works with no signal.

Android From the link

Do this in Chrome — the round red-yellow-green browser app.

  1. Tap Open Power Card above. The game opens in Chrome.
    If it opened inside some other app instead, copy the game’s address — vote-or-fade-away.vercel.app — and paste it into Chrome’s address bar at the top, then tap Go.
  2. Tap the ⋮ three dots menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (on newer phones it may say Install app).
  4. Tap Add. Done!

Power Card now sits on your home screen next to your other apps. Open it from there from now on. Bonus: once it has opened online one time, it even works with no signal.

Got a ZIP file instead? Campfire edition

The ZIP is the offline edition — the whole game in one file package, no internet needed after you have it. It holds two things: the game (index.html) and the DM Guide (module.html), the game-master’s book. Both open the same way on your phone.

On iPhone — one free app first:

  1. Open the App Store, search for Documents by Readdle, and get it. It’s free. (Apple’s own Files app can show the game’s pages but can’t run them — the buttons would do nothing. Documents runs them properly.)
  2. Put the ZIP inside Documents: either download it using the browser built into Documents (the compass button inside the app), or share/send the ZIP to Documents from wherever you received it.
  3. In Documents, tap the ZIP once — it unpacks into a folder.
  4. Open the folder and tap index.html to play. (Game-masters: module.html is your Guide.)

On Android — nothing extra to get:

  1. Open your Files app and tap the ZIP — it unpacks into a folder.
  2. Open the folder, tap index.html, and choose Chrome if it asks how to open it. (Game-masters: module.html is your Guide.)

On a computer?

Just tap Open Power Card above and play in your browser. To keep it, bookmark the page (Ctrl+D, or Cmd+D on a Mac).

If the buttons don’t work

Dead buttons or a frozen page almost always means the game was opened in a preview — the quick look inside Files, Google Drive, or an email/chat app. Previews can show the page but can’t run it. Close the preview and open the game the real way: the link in Safari or Chrome, or the ZIP’s files inside Documents (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
